Research themes

Multiscale immunology. My Ph.D. at Michigan built whole-host and granuloma models of tuberculosis, plus a calibration protocol for them. Details

QSP for biologics and cell therapies. Platform models for TCR-engineered and CAR T cells, ADCs, T cell bispecifics, and engineered cytokines support programs from design to first-in-human dosing. Details

Digital twins. Virtual patients fit to clinical data identify which T cell subsets drive the persistence of engineered T cells. Details

Machine learning for pharmacokinetics. Neural ODEs, sparse regression for ODE discovery, protein language models, and genomics-based prediction of target-mediated drug disposition (LEAP). Details

Agentic modeling. LUCY, a multi-agent framework that designs and calibrates mechanistic models, and open code built on public models. Details
